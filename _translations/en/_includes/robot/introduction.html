A four-wheeled robot with a camera, controlled from your phone's browser: that is what we are building. The ESP32-CAM sends the picture and receives our commands, the L298N drives the four motors, and the DFPlayer Pro handles sound. No separate phone app is needed.

We will start by following the power supply and understanding what each board does. Then we will move on to the wiring, the first firmware upload, and a test with the wheels off the ground. In the second half, we will open the actual code: how a joystick movement reaches the wheels, and what happens when the connection is lost.

This guide covers the 4WD Robot v1.5. The diagrams and complete table remain available for every connection, but you do not have to read all 36 table rows before you can understand the build.

Code excerpts retain the original executable statements; comments are translated for readability.
