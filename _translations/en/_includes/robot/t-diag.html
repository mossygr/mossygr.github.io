Wire lengths in the drawing are not to scale Cut each wire as short as practical. In particular, #11 / #12, the ESP32-CAM's 5V/GND pair: use 0.5mm² wire with a female Dupont terminal, crimped or soldered, under 15 cm. Thin, long wires here cause resets.
How to read the diagram The ESP32-CAM is shown from the back, where the pin names are printed; its camera faces away from you. The L298N is oriented with its heatsink on the right. Each OUT1–OUT4 screw takes two wires, connecting two motors in parallel.
Before turning on the switch
  1. Use a multimeter: no short circuit between WAGO A and WAGO B.
  2. Remove the ESP32-CAM from the Dupont connectors and switch on. Measure 5.0–5.2V at wire #11's Dupont terminal relative to #12. Switch off.
  3. Reconnect the ESP32-CAM: #11 must go to the pin labelled 5V and #12 to the GND immediately beside it. NEVER to 3V3 on the opposite row.
  4. C1's negative stripe faces GND.
  5. First power-on: wheels off the ground.
