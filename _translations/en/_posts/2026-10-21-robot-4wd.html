Downloads and reference images

Download the supplied firmware (.zip) — includes robot_firmware.ino and its six .h files. Change AP_PASSWORD and OTA_PASSWORD before use, as described in the guide.

The MP3 files mentioned in the guide are created or copied by you and are not included in the downloads.

Differences between the guide and the supplied firmware

The guide and both diagrams describe an external DFR0119 amplifier. In the ZIP, comments in robot_config.h and the AT+AMP=ON command in robot_audio.h refer to the DFPlayer Pro's internal amplifier and a speaker directly on its L+/L− outputs. Both sources are preserved as supplied; confirm with the author which arrangement applies to your build.

The original guide mentioned returning to Access Point mode after 10 s, while the supplied robot_config.h defines STA_TIMEOUT_MS 20000, or 20 s. The wire specification 0.5mm² (22AWG) is preserved as supplied and requires technical confirmation from the author.

The diagram, harness, and table are generated from the same data model (tools/model.py) and automatically checked by tools/check.py.

That statement comes from the original source. tools/model.py and tools/check.py were not supplied, and their check has not been run.
